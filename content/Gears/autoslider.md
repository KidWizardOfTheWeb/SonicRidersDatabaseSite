## A Simple, yet effective drifting gear

# About the gear
Auto Slider much similar to Default gear trades out no slipping on ice and reduced speed on jump charge for shorter times drifting. With its reduce drift cap it allows the user to drift more times and keep yourself at near max speeds throughout the track with ease making it a pretty fun alternative to learn how to perform 'Snaking' which is just multiple succesful drift dashes in a row. Affective on all characters it makes itself a staple pick when it comes to playing very spesific characters in the roster.

# Gameplay tips
Character wise this gear can be a monster on All-Rounders and Drift Type Characters since these two types utalize the ability to perform more drifts and/or provide longer drift dashes. But don't count the other types out explore your options and have fun with all the characters in the roster.

 Commonly for Drift Type characters E-10R is the most picked for this gear but others such as E-10Y, E-10B and its light-weight counter part Amy can still use this gear with high efficency in terms of focusing strongly on the drift portion of this gear with their +20 to the drift bonus and -10& drift cost making time spend drifting even shorter than what Autoslider already provides in its base kit.

 All-rounders like Ulala,Cream,GonGon and Eggman do give Auto slider a +10 drift bonus compared to the Drift type's +10 but what they provide over drift that makes them just as good is the +20 Boost Duration on all levels and +2% Boost chain multiplyer. This allows them to drift a bit less compared to the Drift Type but allows them to perform more longer lasting boosts right out of drift and still be able to keep up with its drift-focused counterpart.

 In Combat with all Types at your disposal utalizing your drifts and drift-dashes makes you a very slippery target to hit as a great defensive mechanic and on the offensive side of things being able to attackslide into your opponets at a shorter boost cost makes you a very threatening player, giving you a lot more control on the track than what this gear leads on. Although, not as fast as other gears this gear may struggle against those with natural higher speeds and boosts don't count this gear out with its ability to keep pace going sideways!
