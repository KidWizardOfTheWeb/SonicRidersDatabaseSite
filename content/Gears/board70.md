## The ollie king gear

Based on the arcade skateboard racing game Ollie King this gear brings a fun concept of 'Tricks for Boost' to a whole new level. With Aerodynamic, Ollie Burst and KickDash, these mechanics will help you become the next king and out race any opponent that comes your way!!!

Gear specific details | Special flags
----------------------|--------------
Fuel type             |Air
Special flags         |No speed loss on jump charge, no speed loss on tornado
Mechanics             |Kickdash, ollie Burst, aerodynamic, Level 4 - Air Refill
Frames to charge drift dash|60
## Unique Mechanic: Aerodynamic
Based on your current speed, 7% of said speed is added to the shortcut you use at that moment. This speed is calculated at the moments where you leave to the ground to grind a rail as a speed type, before going off the fly ramp as a fly type or even hitting objects or obstacals as a power type.  

Here is a written example:
* rail speed = 240
* current speed = 250
* final rail speed = rail speed + (current speed * 7%)
* final rail speed 257.5

With this kind of information and how fast you will be going you can rocket through shortcuts around 300 and up to even 600 speed in certain shortcuts when boosting. This can allow you to catch a massive lead on your opponent or even catch up when falling behind at breakneck speeds. 

Board 70's gameplan with using Aerodynamic is to go as fast as possible through your shortcuts while maintaining a good amount of air. You can go really fast depending on your type with speeds from around 380 to even 600+ speed. But, keeping high speeds like that requires a lot of your air gauge and can leave you wide open or being left behind if ignored.

## Unique Mechanic: Ollie Burst
Unique to board 70 when landing a trick, depending on the rank it unlocks an ability to boost once instead of using the kickdash mechanic indicated with a yellow trail.
  * C Rank: 200 speed - 1 frame boost duration
  * B Rank: 210 speed - 60 frame boostduration
  * A Rank: 220 speed - 90 frame boostduration
  * A+ Rank: 225 speed - 90 frame boost duration
  * S Rank: 230 speed - 120 frame boost duration
  * S+ Rank: 235 speed - 180 frame boost duration
  * X Rank: 240 speed - 210 frame boost duration

Characters with the 'All rounders' Typing gain an additional +15 boost duration While characters with the 'Late Boosters' or 'Combat' Typing gain an additional +30 duration.

Ollie burst in gameplay is your way of attacking and catching up in dire situations. Sometimes landing an X rank for the additional air and 240 speed on the burst may not be as important as an S rank depending on the situation at hand when wanting to set up for an attack. This also can be applied for the boost duration as well due to the fact you have to wait for the duration to end to begin using KickDash or holding Jump to cut the boost short and begin KickDashing early. Ollie Burst is truly the battle of Speed Vs Boost Duration that makes the gear very enjoyable to play.

## KickDash
This Allows the use of a 1 Frame Boost and attack stake on the same frame. Speeds from kicksashing are additive instead of a set speed like normal boosts and CANNOT BE BOOST CHAINED. This gear shares the ability to KickDash alongside another gear known as Accelerator. 

Board 70's KickDash Stats:
  * Lvl 1: +50 KickDash Speed - 310 Speed Cap
  * Lvl 2: +65 KickDash Speed - 320 Speed Cap
  * Lvl 3: +80 KickDash Speed - 330 Speed Cap

Kickdash as a whole in game planning allows you to further get close or far away from an opponent as quickly as possible. The 0 frame boost may not put the player into an attack state for long given what is said above but instead allows the player to instantly attack the opponent without warning! KickDashing can also be a very good tool for creating/covering  a massive distance between you and the opponent if used correctly. Similar to JCBC you can spam kickdashes in a rhythmic succession to go high speeds.

## Gear Overview and Showcase
Board 70 as a whole can be played in a variety of ways with most of the characters in the game. Late Boost,Combat, and All-Rounders having the most versatility in using the most out of all of this ExtremeGear's Mechanics. But don't count out the other Archetypes! Top Speed, Trickster and Drifting characters bring a whole new meaning to high speeds making the gear feel insane on sections with long straights or tight corners. Although No Type and Boost Characters aren't the best recommended types for the gear they can still have their use on specific stages making them absolute monsters in the right hands of dedicated racers. Play around these mechanics and you'll even make the Ollie Kings before you envy with your rad tallents.
