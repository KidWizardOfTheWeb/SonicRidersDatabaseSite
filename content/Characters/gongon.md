## Gameplan
GonGon as a character shares the same attack kit with Ai-Ai across all 3 levels with the standard basic attack at Level 1, an AoE Spin-out attack at Level 2, and a Level 3 Trample attack. Use his stregnths of his additional +10 Drift bonus to perform more drifts and drift dashes, extend your boosts and go for attacks longer with his +20 boost duration, and lastly making good use of strengthening your boosts with his +2% Boost Chain Multiplier(BCM). 

# Combat
Combat wise you want to play him in a similar manner to Ai-Ai. Making good use of his attack range at Levels 2 and 3 to force opponents to take alternate routes and conrole power shortcuts.

Offensively: His AoE spinout at level 2 can give a lot of control and can even be used to knock players off course and even ruin their opprotunity to take shortcuts as a whole. His Level 3 Trample can punish players who get to close and stun them for a full 25 frames and can be followed up with a tornado to take their air or even land another solid hit.

These same tactics can be applied defensively too. Utilizing his Level 2 AoE as a form of protection from incoming attackers or even as a tool to scare your opponent into not attacking. The level 3 on the other hand can be used as a very good punishment tool against someone who missed an attack or tries to drop a tornado in front of you.
