## Introduction 
GonGon the power house gorilla smashes his way from Jungle Island. Introduced in patch 2.2 alongside The Debug Menu, Characters Miku and Reala, and the extreme gear known as G.U.N. Gear.He made it his own to prove his strength to all the riders and his longtime rival and friend Ai-Ai. Being similar to Eggman he is the only other All-Rounder Power type on the roster he is no slouch to taking hits and crushing those in front of him with ease.

## Gameplan
GonGon as a character shares the same attack kit as Ai-Ai across all 3 levels with the standard basic attack at Level 1, an AoE Spinnout attack at Level 2, and a devastating Level 3 Trample attack that flattens all that oppose him. Already stated in his introduction hes the only other All-Rounder Power Type alongside Eggman and this makes his approach to combat interesting compared to Ai-Ai who is a Late-Booster. Use his stregnths of his additional +10 Drift bonus on drifting gears, +20 boost duration on gears that specialize in boosting, and his +2% Boost Chain Multiplier(BCM) makes him a force to be reckoned with.
# Combat
Offensively you would want to play him in a similar manner to Ai-Ai. Making good use of his attack range at Levels 2 and 3 to force opponents to take routes and control power paths. His AoE spinout can be pretty devastating when used in the right circumstances, although can still be easily countered on hit by jumping shortly after and using a boost. His Level 3 Trample on the other hand is punishing and in most cases can not be canceled unless near a ramp. When going for attacks try to prevent them from using shortcuts at level 2 and Trample them with your might using the Level 3 attacks far away from any ramps or even wait for them to exit a shortcut and flatten them to the ground with his devastating attack.
These same tacrics can be applied defensovely too. Utilizing his Level 2 AoE as a form of protection from incoming attackers or even as a tool to scare your opponent into not attacking. The level 3 on the other hand can be used as a very good punishment tool against those who are very greedy to land attacks on you. getting trampled by this gorilla is the last thing they will remember while you cross the line to victory.
